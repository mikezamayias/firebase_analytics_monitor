What ships

README hero, compact logo mark, pub.dev screenshot/preview image, OG card, and a small graphics index. The existing demo GIFs stay useful and should be promoted rather than replaced.

What to avoid

No mascots, no glossy gradients, no decorative regional symbols. The Hellas influence is token-level: paper, blue, gold, typography, rules, and archival restraint.
